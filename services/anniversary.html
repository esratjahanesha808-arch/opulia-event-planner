Milestone Events

An Evening That Honours Your Story

Every year of a relationship holds a universe of memory. Our anniversary events are designed to reflect that — intimate candlelit dinners, surprise reveals, private garden parties, or grand celebrations with everyone you love.

Whether you are marking one year or fifty, Opulia creates a space that feels precisely right for your story. Nothing generic. Everything exactly as it should be.

  • Bespoke theme reflecting your shared story
  • Venue selection — intimate or grand
  • Floral and lighting design
  • Surprise reveal coordination
  • Live music and entertainment
  • Private dining with curated menu
How We Work

Our Process

You deserve to be fully present. We handle every detail so you can simply experience the day.

01

Your Story

04

The Evening

You arrive. The rest unfolds perfectly. Your only task is to be present.
