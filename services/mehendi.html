Pre-Wedding Rituals

A Mehendi Night That Feels Like You

We believe a Mehendi night is not just a ritual — it's a celebration of beauty, love, and tradition. Whether intimate and candlelit or vivid and festive, Opulia designs Mehendi evenings that feel entirely, unmistakably yours.

From intimate garden settings to grand marquee evenings — we handle every element: venue, florals, lighting, live music, henna artist curation, food and on-the-day logistics.

  • Fully bespoke theme and aesthetic direction
  • Venue selection and floral transformation
  • Marigold, rose and fairy light installations
  • Live dhol, qawwali or acoustic music curation
  • Henna artist coordination and seating design
  • Complete event-day coordination
