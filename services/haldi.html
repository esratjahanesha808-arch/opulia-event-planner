A Haldi That Feels Like You

We believe a haldi is not just a date — it's a declaration. Whether you're turning 18, 30, 50 or beyond, Opulia designs haldi celebrations that feel entirely, unmistakably yours.

From intimate candlelit dinners to grand parties with hundreds of guests — we handle every element: venue, florals, lighting, entertainment, cake design, food curation, and on-the-day logistics.

  • Fully bespoke theme and aesthetic design

  • Floral and lighting direction
  • Entertainment and live performance curation
  • Custom cake and dessert table design
  • Complete event-day coordination
